The Instinct Tree

Research as a map of scent trails: each research sits in a column by the Pheromone Chamber level it needs, grouped into lanes by branch, with a trail from every research it builds on. Hover (or tap) one to read what it does, what it costs, what it needs and what it opens up. Numbers and the trails between research are samples until the rules session settles them.

    How it reads

    • Left to right is depth. Columns follow the Pheromone Chamber level a research needs; a research that builds on another always sits to its right. The dashed honey line marks how deep your chamber reaches.
    • Lanes are branches (sample: Body, Trails, Colony), so fighting, scouting and growth each read as one line of thought.
    • Trails are pheromone. A laid trail is the violet scent dots from the Pheromone Chamber art; one still to lay is a dashed soil line with the level it needs.
    • Pictures are the same tiles as the list, so nothing new to learn. Greyed with a lock = not reachable yet; honey ring = you can afford it now; violet glow = being learned.

    Touch and hover

    • Hover or focus a research: the card shows what it does, next level's cost and time, what it needs (ticks and crosses, with your levels) and what it leads to. Everything it needs and opens stays lit; the rest fades.
    • Click pins the card so you can move to the Research button. Clicking a name in "Needs" or "Leads to" jumps there.
    • Phone: the same tree, smaller, panning sideways; a tap opens the card as a bottom sheet. The List view stays for quick one-handed research.
    • Keyboard: every research is a button, so Tab walks the tree and Enter pins it.

    Decisions for you

    1. Which view opens first? Recommended: Tree on computers, List on phones (remembered per device once you switch).
    2. Name on screen: "Research" with a Tree switch, or rename the tree the Instinct Tree (as the UI doc already calls it)? Recommended: keep "Research", call the view "Tree".
    3. Hide research you can't reach for two or more steps, OR show the whole tree greyed? Recommended: show it all; seeing the far end is the point of a tree.
    4. Branch lanes: are Body / Trails / Colony the right three? (The rules session decides the final branches; this is only how they'd look.)
