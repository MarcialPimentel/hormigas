# 14 — Monetization

**Version:** 0.1

## Current plan
**None for MVP.** The game must prove it's fun before any monetization is designed.

## Principles (binding for any future monetization)
1. **The fairness test:** if a paying player beats a free player of equal skill and playtime, the purchase is pay-to-win and is not allowed.
2. **No selling resources, speed-ups, units, slots, protection, or research.**
3. **No "convenience" that is actually power.** Extra build queues, extra mission slots, auto-actions that free players can't get — all count as power.
4. **Cosmetics are fine:** nest color themes, ant color palettes, colony banners, profile flair, map markers.
5. **Supporter options are fine:** a one-time "founder" purchase or optional subscription that gives only cosmetics and recognition.
6. **No randomized paid rewards** (no loot boxes).

## Why
The game is inspired by a classic that lost player trust through creeping pay-to-win. Fairness is a pillar (see [00-vision](00-vision.md#design-pillars)) and a selling point.
